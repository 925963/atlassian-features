## Description

Access Rovo directly from your goals to get instant help while you create, manage, and track strategic priorities. This update adds a floating Rovo button to the Goals app, bringing AI-powered insights and search capabilities right to your workflow. To get started: Select Goals from the app swithcer.
	Select the floating Rovo button in the bottom right-hand corner of your screen.
	Select a prompt to ask questions about your goals or the ones you are following.
