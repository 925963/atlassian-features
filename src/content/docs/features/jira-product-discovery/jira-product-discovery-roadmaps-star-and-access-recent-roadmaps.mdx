## Description

Jira Product Discovery users can now star their key roadmaps and quickly access recent ones directly from the sidebar. This update helps you stay organized and save time by providing faster navigation to the roadmaps you use most frequently. Open Jira Product Discovery
	Go to your roadmap.
	Recent opened roadmap will appear in the Recent section.
	To star roadmap select the More actions menu (⋯) next to the roadmap name.
	Select Add to starred to move it to your Starred section, or use the star
