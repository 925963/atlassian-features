## Description

You can now add visual covers to cards in the board view. This change allows you to personalize your ideas with images from attachments, Unsplash, or solid colors, making it easier to give each idea card a distinct visual identity, making faster to scan a board column, spot a specific idea, and orient yourself in a large backlog. To add visual covers to cards: Open any board view in Jira Product Discovery.
	Go to Display settings and switch on the Card cover images toggle.
	Open the idea card me
