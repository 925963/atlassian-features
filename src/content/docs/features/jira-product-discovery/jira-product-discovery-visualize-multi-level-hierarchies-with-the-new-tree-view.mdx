## Description

Jira Product Discovery Premium now includes a Tree view to help you organize ideas into multi-level custom hierarchies. This view type allows you to better understand the relationships between different items and manage complex product roadmaps with ease, including roadmap planning and cross-space idea linking through connection fields.
