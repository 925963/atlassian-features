## Description

Loom meeting recap emails now include more context and interactive elements to help you stay informed after your meetings. These enhancements provide a clearer view of who attended, what was shared, and offer a way to provide feedback on the accuracy of your AI-generated recaps. This change is available for users on Enterprise, Business + AI, or trial plans with AI enabled. Record a meeting using Loom.
