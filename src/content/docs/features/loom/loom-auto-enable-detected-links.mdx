## Description

Loom now automatically enables the auto-link feature when it detects the Loom Chrome extension. This change helps you save time by instantly turning Loom links into embedded video players across your favorite apps without manual setup. You can opt out of this behavior at any time in your settings. Open the Loom app or the Loom Chrome extension.
