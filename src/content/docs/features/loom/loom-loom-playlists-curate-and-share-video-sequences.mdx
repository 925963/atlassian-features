## Description

Loom users on paid plans can now create and share curated playlists to streamline workflows like new-hire onboarding and product training. This change allows viewers to follow a specific sequence of videos through a single, shareable link, providing a more structured and intentional viewing experience in Loom. To get started with this change Go to the Playlists home page in your Loom workspace.
