## Description

Workspace admins in Loom can now connect calendars for their entire workspace and set a recording default for users. This change helps teams automate their meeting recording workflows and ensures consistency across the app. To get started with this change Log in to your Loom workspace.
	Go to Settings, then Workspace settings.
	Select Calendar connect to link your calendar.
	Choose your preferred recording default for all users in the workspace.
