## Description

Jira Service Management now supports space backgrounds, allowing you to customize the look and feel of your workspace. This update brings a more personalized experience, helping you distinguish between different spaces and making your daily work environment more engaging. Open your service space in Jira Service Management.
	Select the More actions (•••) menu for the space, then select Set space background.
	Select a background color or upload an image to apply it to your space.
