## Description

You can now reorder your starred items in the Jira sidebar, making it easier to organize your most-used links for faster access. This update helps you personalize your Jira experience and quickly find what matters most. To get started with this change: Go to your Jira sidebar.
	Select and drag any starred item to your preferred position in the list.
