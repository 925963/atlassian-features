## Description

Save time and reduce cognitive load during incidents by automating your external communications. Jira Service Management now includes a native automation action that creates and updates status page incidents directly from your incident workflow. This ensures your stakeholders stay informed in real-time without requiring manual context-switching from responders. To get started with this change: Go to Space settings, then Automation.
