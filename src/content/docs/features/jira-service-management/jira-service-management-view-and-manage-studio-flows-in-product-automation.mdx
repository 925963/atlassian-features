## Description

Manage your automation flows more effectively with the new ability to view Studio flows directly within Product Automation. This update provides a centralized view of your flows, making it easier to track and organize automated processes across your Atlassian apps. Open your Atlassian app and navigate to Product Automation.
	Select the Studio flows tab from the automation list.
	Review the list of available flows..
