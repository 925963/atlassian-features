## Description

Jira Service Management now supports RovoOps agent in Slack This update allows your team to use AI-powered agents to automate tasks and find information directly from your chat app, helping you resolve incidents and requests faster. Connect an incident or an alert to an existing channel or create a new one.
	The Rovo Slack app gets added to your incident/alert channel with RovoOps configured.
	You can chat with the RovoOps in these channels with @Rovo queries.
