## Description

Agents can now access all AI assistance from a single Resolve with Rovo panel in the work item view. This unified experience automatically surfaces the most relevant AI-powered help—like Rovo-generated resolution plans or suggested actions—based on the context of the request. This simplifies workflows and helps agents resolve issues faster without switching between different sections. To view this change: Open a service space in Jira Service Management.
