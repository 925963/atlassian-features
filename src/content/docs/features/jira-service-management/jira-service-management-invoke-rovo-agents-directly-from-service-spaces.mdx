## Description

Service desk agents can now find and invoke Rovo agents directly within Jira Service Management. This update brings the AI agent experience from Jira to service spaces, so you get AI assistance without leaving your work item. Use agents to draft responses, summarise complex requests, or perform specialized tasks where you work. Open any work item in your Jira Service Management space.
