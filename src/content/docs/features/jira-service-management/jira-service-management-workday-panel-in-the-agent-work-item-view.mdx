## Description

Agents working on HR service management spaces in Jira Service Management can now view Workday employee data for a reporter directly within the work item. This collapsible panel surfaces essential context, such as work schedules and time-off summaries, helping agents resolve HR requests more efficiently without switching between apps. To view the Workday section: Open a work item in an HR Service Management space.
