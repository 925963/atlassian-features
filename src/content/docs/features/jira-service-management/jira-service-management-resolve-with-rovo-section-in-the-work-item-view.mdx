## Description

Jira Service Management now includes an Resolve with Rovo summary section in the work item view to help agents quickly understand the context of a request. This feature uses Atlassian Intelligence to condense long conversations and internal notes into a concise overview. This allows teams to respond to customers faster and resolve requests without reading through every update. It also includes automated resolution suggestions when available.
