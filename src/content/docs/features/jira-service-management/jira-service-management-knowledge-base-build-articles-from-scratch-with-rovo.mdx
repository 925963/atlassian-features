## Description

Remove the onboarding friction of setting up a knowledge base in Jira Service Management. This new feature lets you add a knowledge base to your space effortlessly. Atlassian Rovo generates draft articles for you to review and publishes them to a Confluence space, so your customers can start finding answers immediately without agents needing to write articles from scratch. To generate a knowlege based article using Rovo: Go to your service space and select Knowledge base from the sidebar.
