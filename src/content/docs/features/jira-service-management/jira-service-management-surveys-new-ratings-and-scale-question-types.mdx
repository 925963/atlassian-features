## Description

Jira Service Management surveys now include ratings and scale question types, giving you more ways to collect feedback across your organization. You can add these new question types to your surveys using the survey builder. To add new question types: Go to your service space and select Surveys. Make sure you’ve activated surveys, in Features.
	Select the survey you want to edit or create a new one.
	Select Add field then drag and drop the Ratings or Scale question types into your survey.
