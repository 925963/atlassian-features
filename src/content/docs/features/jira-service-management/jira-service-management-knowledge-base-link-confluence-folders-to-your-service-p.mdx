## Description

Project admins can now link individual Confluence folders to a Jira Service Management knowledge base. This allows for finer control over the content surfaced to agents and help seekers by linking specific sub-sections of a space rather than the entire space. This feature supports both native Confluence folders and cross-site folders within the same Atlassian organization. Go to your service project and select Space settings, then Knowledge base.
