## Description

Save time and maintain context by sharing your Rovo Chat conversations with others in your organization. Instead of copying text or taking screenshots, you can now generate a secure link to share a specific point in your chat history, allowing colleagues to view the discussion or use it as a starting point for their own work in Atlassian Rovo. To get started with this change Open a conversation in Rovo Chat that you want to share.
