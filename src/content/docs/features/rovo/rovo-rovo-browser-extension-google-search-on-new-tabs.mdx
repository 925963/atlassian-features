## Description

You can now toggle Google search on of off from your new tab. This give you more control over your workspace, so you can focus on Rovo Search or keep your preferred search engine within reach. Open a new tab in your web browser.
	In the search filter of the page, select the Google tab to navigate to Google search.
	Type your query in the search box and press the Enter key to search. To make Google search your default search, select the Settings icon in the navigation bar and in the New tab page
