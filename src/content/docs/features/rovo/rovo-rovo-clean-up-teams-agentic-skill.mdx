## Description

Keep your organization organized by using Rovo to identify and archive inactive or redundant teams. This new agentic skill in Rovo helps admins maintain a clear and accurate view of their team structure with minimal manual effort. Open Rovo from the app switcher or your Atlassian Home.
	Type /clean-up-teams to surface the Clean up teams skill. You can also find a nudge in the teams directory to clean up teams, or select More actions (•••) to launch the skill.
