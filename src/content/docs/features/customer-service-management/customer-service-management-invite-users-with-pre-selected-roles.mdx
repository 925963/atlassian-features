## Description

Space admins can save time when adding new team members to Jira Service Management and Customer Service Management. We've updated the invite modal to automatically pre-select the most likely role based on where you are in the app, ensuring new users get the right access and permissions from day one. To invite users with pre-selected roles: Go to your service space.
