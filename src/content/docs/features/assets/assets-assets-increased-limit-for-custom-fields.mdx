## Description

The Assets custom field now supports linking up to 200 objects per field on Jira work item screens (create, edit, view, and transition), Jira Service Management portal and forms, and work item history - a 10× increase from the previous limit of 20. On Jira Service Management queues, list view, board and backlog views, and gadgets and dashboards, the limit increases from 20 to 50.
