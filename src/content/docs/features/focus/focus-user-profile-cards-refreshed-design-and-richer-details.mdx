## Description

We've updated user profile cards across Atlassian apps to provide a more modern experience and help you connect with your teammates faster. The refreshed design includes new sections for recent work, reporting lines, and direct-message actions for Slack or Microsoft Teams, making it easier to understand who someone is and what they're working on without leaving your current page.
