## Description

Admins can now combine multiple teams into a single team within Atlassian administration. This helps you clean up duplicate teams and ensure your organization remains organized and easy to navigate. Merge teams Go to Atlassian administration.
	Select Directory, then Teams.
	Choose the teams you want to combine and select Merge team.
