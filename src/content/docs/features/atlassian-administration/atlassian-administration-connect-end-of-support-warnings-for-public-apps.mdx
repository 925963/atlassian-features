## Description

Identify which public apps are affected by the upcoming Connect end-of-support milestone with new in-app warnings. These messages provide guidance on migrating to Forge to help you avoid potential service interruptions. To view the list of impacted apps: Go to Atlassian Administration. Select your organization if you have more than one.
