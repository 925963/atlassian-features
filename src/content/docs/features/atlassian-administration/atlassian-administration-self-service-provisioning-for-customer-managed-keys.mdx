## Description

Organization admins can now add Enterprise apps (Jira, Confluence, Jira Service Management) that use customer-managed keys (CMK) encryption directly from Atlassian Administration. If your organization has CMK configured, you can now choose between Atlassian-managed keys or your customer-managed keys while setting up your app. To add a supported app with your encryption policy: Log in to admin.atlassian.com.
	Select your organization.
	Go to Apps, then select Atlassian apps.
	Select Add app.
