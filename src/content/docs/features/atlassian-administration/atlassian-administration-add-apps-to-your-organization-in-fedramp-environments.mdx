## Description

Organization admins can now add Jira, Confluence, and Jira Service Management apps to their organization in FedRAMP environments, without contacting Atlassian Support. Grow your secure environment as your needs change, and stay compliant with FedRAMP requirements. Go to Atlassian Administration and select your organization.
	Select Apps, then Atlassian apps, then Add app.
