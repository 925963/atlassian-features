## Description

We've refreshed the calendars you embed on your Confluence pages so they look and feel just like Space Calendars. We’ve also improved loading performance to reduce flicker and layout shifts as your pages load. Together, these changes make for a more consistent and polished calendar experience across Confluence. To check out these changes: Open any Confluence page or live doc that already has a calendar embedded, or create a new one.
	Existing calendars will automatically show the refreshed view
