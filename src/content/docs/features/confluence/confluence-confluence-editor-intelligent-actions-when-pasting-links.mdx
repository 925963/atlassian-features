## Description

Confluence now offers intelligent, context-aware actions when you paste links into the editor. When you paste links, a menu will suggest specific actions to help you refine your work, such as summarizing linked content or changing its display. These AI-powered suggestions help you polish your docs and save time by automating common editing tasks directly at the moment of paste.
