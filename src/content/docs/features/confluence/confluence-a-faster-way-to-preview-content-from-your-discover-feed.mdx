## Description

We’re testing a new way for you to catch up on trending work without losing your place. When you select a Confluence published page or blog from your Discover feed, it will now open in a preview panel on the right side of your screen. This allows you to quickly scan and read updates while staying in your Home browsing flow. If you need a full view, you can still choose to open the content in a new tab from the panel header.
