## Description

Implicit content creator permissions and privileges — including archiving and managing restrictions — now belong to the content owner rather than the original creator. Transferring ownership also transfers these privileges, giving admins greater control over content governance. Go to a content item where you are the owner but not the original creator.
	Here, you can:
	
		Update content restrictions.
		Archive the content item.
