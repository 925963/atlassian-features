## Description

We've updated the design and layout of empty states on the Confluence Home For you tab to provide a more modern and helpful experience. These changes include a refresh of the Pick up where you left off section and the panels for Following, Popular, and Calendar. We've also tidied up the Announcements tab to reduce clutter and help you focus on your most relevant work. In Confluence, go to Home.
	Select the For you tab.
	View the updated designs in the empty state sections.
