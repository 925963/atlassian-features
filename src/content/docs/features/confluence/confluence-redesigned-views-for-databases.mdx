## Description

Configure your database views without losing sight of your data. We’ve replaced full-height side panels with contextual menus for filtering, sorting, grouping, and layout settings. These compact menus appear directly below the toolbar, making it faster and less interruptive to adjust your view while you work. To get started with this change: Open a database in Confluence.
