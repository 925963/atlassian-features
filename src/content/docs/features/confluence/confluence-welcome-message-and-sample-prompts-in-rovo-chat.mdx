## Description

New users in Confluence can now get started with Atlassian Rovo more quickly through a personalized welcome message and sample prompt. When new users enter a space, Rovo Chat will automatically open to introduce you to its capabilities and provide a ready-to-use prompt, helping you find information and learn about your organization with a single click. Be a new user who created their account less than 14 days ago.
