## Description

Manage space access using role-based access control. Instead of selecting 14 individual checkboxes for every person, you can now assign a single role to grant the right permissions instantly. This change brings consistency across your spaces and makes it simpler to scale access as your organization grows. To update your site default access for new spaces: Go to Confluence administration.
