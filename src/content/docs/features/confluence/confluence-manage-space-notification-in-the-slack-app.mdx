## Description

We're removing the Slack Notifications tab from space settings in Confluence Cloud. This change deprecates the user interface while ensuring that your notification delivery remains unaffected. You can continue to manage your space notification settings directly within the Slack app. To manage your space notifications: Open your space in Confluence.
	Select Space settings from the sidebar.
	Select Integrations to view your available app settings.
	To manage your notifications, open the Slack app
