## Description

Save time in the editor by quickly converting short phrases into a status. When a paragraph or heading has less than or equal to 30 characters of texts, you can now transform it into a status component to highlight important information in your content To get started with this change: Open the editor in Confluence or Jira
	Type a short phrase of up to 30 characters on a new line.
	Click the drag handle next to the text you just typed
