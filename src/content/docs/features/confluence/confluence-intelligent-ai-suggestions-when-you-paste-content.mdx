## Description

Save time and improve your content in Confluence with intelligent AI suggestions that appear when you paste text. When you paste content longer than 100 characters, a menu will suggest specific actions to help you polish, remix, or refine your writing using Rovo. Copy text from an internal or external source that is at least 100 characters long.
	Paste the content into the Confluence editor.
	Select an action from the menu that appears, such as Add polish, Improve writing, or Ask Rovo.
