## Description

New users of the Confluence mobile app will now receive a series of push notifications during their first five days to help them discover high-value features. These notifications provide direct links to key capabilities like audio briefings, search, and content creation, making it easier to stay productive while away from a laptop. To view and manage notifications: Sign in to the Confluence mobile app for the first time.
	Ensure push notifications are enabled for the app in your device settings.
