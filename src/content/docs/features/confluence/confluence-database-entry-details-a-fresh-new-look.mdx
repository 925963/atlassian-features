## Description

We've updated the look of the entry details panel in Confluence databases to provide a more modern and streamlined experience. This redesign makes it easier to view and manage your database information across the standard panel and supported embedded surfaces in Confluence. Open a Confluence database.
	Select an entry or a specific cell within the database.
	Open the entry details panel to see the updated design.
