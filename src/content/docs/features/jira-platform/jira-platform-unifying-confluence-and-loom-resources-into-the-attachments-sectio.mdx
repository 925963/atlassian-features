## Description

Jira now brings Confluence pages and Loom videos into one unified Attachments section, making it easier to manage all your resources in one place. This streamlined experience reduces clutter and helps teams collaborate more efficiently by providing quick access to important documents and videos. To use the new Attachments section: Open a Jira work item.
