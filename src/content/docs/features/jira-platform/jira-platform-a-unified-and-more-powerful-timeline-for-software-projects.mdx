## Description

We’re upgrading the Jira timeline to help you plan and track work with more clarity. This update brings a consistent experience across your projects, making it easier to manage everything from high-level epics to individual subtasks in one view. You can now view and manage all work items, including subtasks and items not linked to an epic, directly on your timeline.
