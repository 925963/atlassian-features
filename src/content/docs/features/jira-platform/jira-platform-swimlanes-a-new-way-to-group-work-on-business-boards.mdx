## Description

We're introducing swimlanes to business boards in Jira to help you better visualize and manage your team's work. This change replaces column-based grouping with horizontal swimlanes, allowing you to keep your columns fixed to status while grouping work by other meaningful dimensions, such as assignee, priority, or category.
