## Description

Save time and streamline your workflow with an enhanced Marketplace experience in Jira and Confluence. The new information architecture and tile system make it easier to discover apps and find the information you need. Open the Jira or Confluence app.
	Select Apps in the left-side navigation bar.
	Choose Explore More apps to explore the new experience.
