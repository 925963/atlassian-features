## Description

Save time and reduce manual effort by using Atlassian Intelligence to break down work items into smaller, manageable units directly within your plan. This feature uses Rovo to suggest child work items based on the context of your parent item, helping you plan more efficiently and maintain momentum as your projects scale. Open a plan in Jira and go to the Scope section of your timeline.
	Select More actions (•••) for the work item you want to break down.
	Select Suggest child work items.
	Review
