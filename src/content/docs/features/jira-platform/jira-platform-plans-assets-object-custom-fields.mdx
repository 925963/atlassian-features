## Description

You can now add Asset Objects custom fields to your plans in Jira. This update allows you to view, edit, group, and filter by Assets values directly on work items within a plan, providing better visibility and control over your Assets during the planning process. Open your plan in Jira.
	Go to Plan Settings, then Timeline.
	Choose Add field and select the Asset Objects field you want to include.
