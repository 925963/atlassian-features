## Description

You can now view Atlas projects associated with work items directly within your plans in Jira. This integration allows you to see which Atlas projects are linked to your work items, helping you align your team's work with broader strategic initiatives without leaving your plan. To get started with this change: Open a plan in Jira.
	Select Fields, then Add a field.
