## Description

Filter work items To use the new filtering experience: Open a business board, business timeline, or calendar in Jira.
	Select the Filters menu at the top of the view.
	Choose the criteria you want to filter by, such as Assignee, Status, or Priority.
	Select multiple values within a category to refine your results instantly.
