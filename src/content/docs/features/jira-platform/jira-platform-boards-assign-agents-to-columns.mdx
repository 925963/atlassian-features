## Description

Save time by assigning agents to workflow columns directly from your board in Jira. This update allows you to manage agent assignments more quickly in team-managed spaces without leaving your current view. You can also hover over an agent’s avatar to see their details at a glance. Go to your board in a team-managed board in Jira.
