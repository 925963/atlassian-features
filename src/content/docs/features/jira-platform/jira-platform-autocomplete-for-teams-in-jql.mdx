## Description

Find and select Atlassian teams more easily when using the membersOf() function in Jira Query Language (JQL). An autocomplete dropdown now lists your site's teams as you type, so you no longer need to manually find and enter team IDs. Filter work items by team name to ensure your boards and reports stay accurate. To get started with this change: 1. Open your Jira app and go to your space.
