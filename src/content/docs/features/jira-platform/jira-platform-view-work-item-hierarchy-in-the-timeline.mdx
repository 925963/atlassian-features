## Description

Jira users can now view and navigate work item hierarchy directly from the timeline when filters are applied. This change introduces a hierarchy flyout that helps you understand the relationship between parent and child work items without losing your place in the timeline. To view the work item hierarchy: Go to the Timeline view in your Jira space.
	Apply a filter to your work items.
	Select Show hierarchy on an work item row.
