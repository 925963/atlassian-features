## Description

Save time and improve visibility by adding more fields to your timeline in Jira. You can now include Updated, Created, Read-only text and Paragraph fields directly on the Plan to keep your team informed without switching views. To get started with this change Open a plan in Jira.
	Go to Plan settings, then Timeline.
	Select Add field.
	Choose the read-only text or paragraph field you want to add.
	Select Add to see the field on your plan.
	Go to the Timeline and select Add Fields to show Read-on
