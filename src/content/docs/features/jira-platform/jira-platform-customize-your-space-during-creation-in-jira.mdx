## Description

You can now customize your space when using the team-managed Kanban template in Jira. Streamline your setup process by pre-configuring work types, setting column names, and adding your teammates to get your team up and running faster. To customize your space: Select Spaces > Create space in the navigation
	Under space templates, select the Kanban template
	Select Customize
