## Description

Enjoy a more consistent experience when managing your work for business spaces in Jira. When you select a cell in the Comments column, work items now open in the preview pane to match other views across the app. To view this change: Open your business space in Jira and select List.
	Locate the Comments column for any work item.
	Select the comment icon or text in that column to open the preview pane.
