## Description

Plan work more effectively by managing the capacity of individual team members in Jira. This new experience allows you to see each person's availability and workload in one place, helping you balance tasks and prevent burnout across your team. Go to your space in Jira.
	Select the Add tab icon (+) in the horizontal navigation.
	Select the Capacity tab to add it to your navigation.
	Select Capacity to interact with the capacity planner.
