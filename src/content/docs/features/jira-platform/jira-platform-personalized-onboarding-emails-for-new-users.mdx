## Description

Onboard to Jira with tailored tips delivered straight to your inbox. If you create a work item on your first day, we’ll send you a personalized email the next business day. This email highlights one of your incomplete task and offers up to five smart suggestions to help you add context and make your work more actionable. Open Jira and create a new work item on your first day.
	Ensure the work item is incomplete (for example, it's missing a description or hasn't been assigned).
	Check your email
